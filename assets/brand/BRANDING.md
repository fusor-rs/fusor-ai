# fusor-ai identity

fusor-ai follows [fusor's identity](https://github.com/fusor-rs/fusor/blob/main/apps/landing/BRANDING.md),
as hypercmd and hyperpane do: the same palette, wordmark typeface and two-piece
construction.

The mark is fusor's F with its lower shape struck as a lightning bolt. The upper
shape is fusor's own. The bolt keeps the top edge and rounded corner of the F's
middle arm, so the gap between the two pieces is fusor's; its notch and its
spike's inner edge are cut on the same diagonal as the F's arms, and its tip
ends on the F's baseline. The wordmark is Lexend SemiBold, outlined to paths.
All logo SVGs contain vector paths, with no embedded raster images, external
fonts, or scripts.

| Use | Artwork |
| --- | --- |
| Symbol in color | [fusor-ai.svg](fusor-ai.svg) |
| Horizontal logo | [Light backgrounds](fusor-ai-horizontal.svg), [dark backgrounds](fusor-ai-horizontal-dark.svg) |

Use the symbol alone for small icons and avatars. Use the dark-background
horizontal logo where the page background is dark; it keeps the symbol in
color and switches the wordmark to warm white.

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Upper shape | Vermilion `#DF5036` |
| Bolt | Oxide `#A83228` |
| Wordmark on dark backgrounds | Warm white `#FAF9F6` |
| Wordmark | Charcoal `#272A2C` |

Preserve the proportions, the gap between the two pieces, and clear space.
